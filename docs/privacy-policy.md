# AI Personal Call Agent - Privacy Policy (Template / Pre-Deployment Draft)

> **IMPORTANT LEGAL NOTICE:**
> This document is a privacy framework template provided for the AI Personal Call Agent system. It does **not** constitute legal advice. Call recording, caller disclosure, transcription, artificial intelligence processing, and biometric/voice privacy requirements vary significantly by country, state, and jurisdiction (e.g., one-party vs. two-party/all-party consent laws in the United States, GDPR in the European Union, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in India, and national telecommunications regulations).
> 
> The system owner is solely responsible for consulting qualified legal counsel and configuring the application's consent mode, disclosure prompts, and retention policies to comply with applicable local laws prior to production deployment.

---

## 1. Overview & System Purpose

The **AI Personal Call Agent** is a personal telephony receptionist software system designed to answer incoming phone calls routed to a dedicated cloud virtual number, identify itself to callers as an artificial intelligence assistant, understand caller intent, evaluate call urgency, notify the system owner of urgent matters via SMS and push notifications, and present call records on an authorized control panel.

---

## 2. Information Collected & Processed

Depending on owner settings and caller consent, the system processes and may store the following categories of data:

### A. Core Call Metadata (Always Processed)
* **Caller Phone Number:** Extracted from telecommunications signaling (Caller ID / ANI).
* **AI Virtual Number:** The dialed cloud telephone number.
* **Call Timing & Metrics:** Start timestamp, end timestamp, call duration, and termination status.
* **Urgency Classification:** Automated urgency level (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL) and the operational rationale.
* **High-Level Call Summary:** Brief factual summary generated by the AI to inform the owner.

### B. Conversational Audio (Transient Processing)
* Live caller speech is streamed temporarily to speech recognition and AI language model APIs to conduct natural two-way conversation.
* **Raw Audio Recording:** **DISABLED by default**. Audio is **never recorded or stored persistently** unless the owner explicitly turns Recording ON **and** the caller grants consent in accordance with the configured jurisdiction policy.

### C. Conversational Transcripts (Optional)
* **Persistent Text Transcripts:** **DISABLED by default**. Full turn-by-turn text transcripts are not persisted to storage unless explicitly enabled by the owner and permitted by the configured consent mode.

### D. Owner Information
* Owner email, encrypted authentication credentials, authorized mobile phone number (for SMS alerts), and device tokens for Firebase Cloud Messaging.

## 3. Mandatory AI Caller Disclosure & Consent Framework

The system incorporates technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized or clandestine recording:

1. **AI Identity Disclosure:** The assistant begins every call by clearly disclosing its AI identity:
   > *"Hello, you've reached Manish's AI assistant. I'm an AI assistant helping manage his calls. How can I help?"*
   If asked *"Are you a real person?"*, the AI explicitly clarifies: *"No, I'm an AI assistant helping manage Manish's calls."*
2. **Configurable Consent Modes:**
   * `DISABLE_RECORDING_AND_TRANSCRIPTION` (**Default MVP setting**): Live processing operates transiently without saving audio or text transcripts.
   * `DISCLOSURE_ONLY`: Informs caller before conversation proceeds.
   * `ASK_FOR_CONSENT`: Plays the disclosure and prompts for affirmative verbal or DTMF consent. If the caller declines, persistent recording and transcription are suppressed while continuing call handling where technically feasible.
3. **No-Consent Handling:** If consent is declined, no persistent recording or transcript is created. Only minimal operational metadata (caller number, duration, urgency, brief redacted summary) is retained.
4. **Consent Logging:** Consent decisions are recorded with timestamps, policy versions, and status (`NOT_REQUIRED`, `PENDING`, `GRANTED`, `DECLINED`).

---

## 4. AI Data Minimization & Confidentiality Safeguards

* **No Credential Solicitation:** The AI prompt strictly forbids asking callers for passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, banking credentials, credit card numbers, or sensitive government identification.
* **Automated Redaction:** Summaries are processed to filter and redact inadvertently spoken credentials before persistence.
* **SMS Privacy Boundaries:** Urgent SMS notifications contain only minimal operational details (caller name, urgency, brief reason, and callback request). Complete transcripts and sensitive parameters are **never** transmitted over unencrypted SMS.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers & Subprocessors

* **Cloud Telephony (Exotel):** Handles telephony signaling, call answering, and optional call recording storage according to Exotel's data protection agreements.
* **Artificial Intelligence (OpenAI API):** Processes speech and conversational text for response generation and urgency classification under API data confidentiality terms.
* **Backend & Database (Firebase / Google Cloud):**
  * **Firebase Authentication:** Handles owner identity tokens.
  * **Cloud Firestore:** Stores structured call records, alerts, and settings in tenant-isolated user paths (`users/{userId}/...`).
  * **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** Delivers encrypted push alerts to the owner's Android device.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Automatic Deletion

The owner configures strict retention limits through the Privacy & Retention panel:
* **Call Metadata:** Default 90 days (configurable: 7, 30, 90, 180, 365 days).
* **Transcripts:** Default DISABLED.
* **Audio Recordings:** Default DISABLED.
* **Urgent Alerts:** Default 90 days.

An automated backend maintenance worker periodically scans and purges records exceeding their configured retention lifecycle.

---

## 7. Owner Control & Data Rights

The authenticated owner maintains full administrative control via the Android application:
* **Manual Individual Deletion:** Delete specific calls, alerts, transcripts, or recordings on demand.
* **Bulk Data Purge:** Permanently remove all stored call history with confirmation dialogs.
* **Export My Data:** Download all call metadata, urgency analyses, and summaries in structured JSON format.
* **Audit Trail:** Review security logs of sensitive administrative actions (policy updates, recording toggles, deletion events).
